A study timer that only counts while your phone is face down

Most study timers keep running while you scroll. If your phone is always in your hand while you study, the number on the timer stops meaning anything. Studydown works the other way: flip your phone face down to start counting, pick it up and the count pauses. This guide shows how to use it as a study timer and study time tracker, and how to turn exam deadlines into a daily pace you can actually hit.

How face-down timing works

  1. Choose an activity, such as a subject, a reading habit or language practice.
  2. Tap to start, then place your phone face down on a steady surface.
  3. Keep Studydown open while timing. Picking up your phone pauses the count after your chosen grace period.
  4. Leaving the app stops the count, so background time is never recorded as study time.

Every face-down session keeps a Focus Signature: how many times you picked up your phone and your average uninterrupted time.

Track study hours by subject

  • Create, color and arrange your own activities, one per subject or project.
  • See today and this week, consecutive days and a breakdown by activity.
  • Add missed time or correct entries manually.
  • Save records as CSV.

Turn an exam deadline into a daily pace

Give each deadline an effort target. Studydown combines it with the activity time you have logged and shows the daily pace still required. Set weekly goals too, with reminders every day or every few hours.

Frequently asked questions

Does Studydown block other apps?

No. It records time while your phone is face down and Studydown is open; leaving the app stops the count.

What happens if I pick up my phone?

The count pauses after the grace period you choose, and the pickup is counted in your Focus Signature.

Can I track several subjects?

Yes. Create your own activities with names and colors and see a breakdown by activity.

I forgot to start the timer. Can I add time?

Yes. You can add missed time or correct entries manually.

Do I need an account?

No. Timing and records stay on your device, with no account or ads.

Is Studydown a subscription?

No. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited activities and recording; there is no subscription.

Studydown is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple. It records time while your phone is face down and the app is open; it does not block other apps.
