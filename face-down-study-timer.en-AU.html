How face-down timing works

  1. Choose an activity: Maths Advanced, English, a uni unit or anything else you study.
  2. Tap to start, then rest your phone face down on a steady surface.
  3. Keep Studydown open while timing. Picking the phone up pauses the count after your chosen grace period.
  4. Leaving the app stops time from being added.

Your stats also show how often you picked up your phone and how long you stayed focused.

Log study hours for each subject

  • Name and colour your activities and arrange them your way.
  • Review today and this week, your consecutive days and the time spent on each activity.
  • Add or correct entries manually.

Frequently asked questions

Can Studydown tell me how much to study each day before my HSC or VCE exams?

Set a deadline and a target, and Studydown shows the daily pace you still need, based on the time you have logged.

Does the timer keep going if I check my phone?

No. Picking the phone up pauses the count after your chosen grace period.

Can I log each subject separately?

Yes. Name and colour an activity for each subject and see the time spent on each one.

I studied but forgot to start the timer. What now?

You can add or correct entries manually.

Can I get study reminders?

Yes. Choose a daily reminder or one every few hours, alongside weekly goals and deadlines.

Is there a subscription?

No. A single Pro purchase unlocks unlimited activities and recording, advanced statistics, goals, deadlines, widgets, Apple Watch and full backups.

Do I have to sign up?

No. Timing and records stay on your device, without an account or ads.

What if I leave the app during a session?

Keep Studydown open while timing; leaving the app stops time from being added.

Can I back up my study records?

Save your records as CSV. Pro also includes JSON backup and restore.
