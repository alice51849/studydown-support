A face-down timer for revision that only counts real focus time

Revising for GCSEs or A-levels with your phone on the desk? A normal stopwatch keeps running while you reply to messages. Studydown only counts while your phone is face down: flip it to start, pick it up and it pauses, so only real focus time counts towards your revision hours. Here is how to use it to track revision by subject and pace yourself towards exam dates.

How the face-down timer works

  1. Select an activity, such as a GCSE subject, an A-level module or a uni essay.
  2. Tap to start and rest your phone on a steady surface, screen down.
  3. Keep Studydown open while timing. When you pick the phone up, counting pauses after your chosen grace period.
  4. Leaving the app stops further time being recorded, so time spent elsewhere is never logged as revision.

You can also see how often you picked up your phone and how long you stayed focused.

Organise revision by subject

  • Name, colour and arrange activities to suit your day: one per subject, paper or project.
  • Review today, this week, consecutive days and how your time is split between activities.
  • Add or amend entries manually if you forgot to start the timer.
  • Save records in CSV format.

Count down to exam season

Mocks over winter, exams in May and June: set a deadline and a target, and Studydown shows the daily pace you still need. Add weekly goals and choose daily reminders or an interval of several hours.

Frequently asked questions

Can I split my revision time across GCSE or A-level subjects?

Yes. Create an activity for each subject, give it a name and colour, and review how your time is split between activities today and this week.

What happens if I pick up my phone mid-session?

Counting pauses after the grace period you choose, and Studydown shows how often you picked up your phone.

Will Studydown remind me to revise?

Yes. Set weekly goals and deadlines, then choose daily reminders or an interval of several hours.

Does time keep counting if I switch to another app?

No. Keep Studydown open while timing; leaving the app stops further time being recorded.

Where should my phone go while the timer runs?

Rest it screen down on a steady surface, such as your desk, with Studydown open.

I forgot to start the timer. Can I add the time later?

Yes. You can add or amend entries manually.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Timing and records stay on your device, with no account and no ads.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. A one-off Pro purchase unlocks unlimited activities and recording, advanced statistics, goals, deadlines, widgets, Apple Watch and backups.

Can I export my revision hours?

Yes. Save records in CSV format. Pro also provides JSON backup and restore.
